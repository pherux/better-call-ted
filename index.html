06 / A few things on the record

FAIR
QUESTIONS.

Straight answers.
No dramatic courtroom monologue.

Is this for me or my business?+

Either. Ted works on personal brands for founders, executives, and independent professionals, and business brands that need a clearer story and a stronger presence. The plan starts with your goals.

Can you guarantee I'll go viral?+

No. Anyone promising that is selling you a lottery ticket in a blazer. We can build stronger positioning, more compelling content, and a repeatable plan. Reach and results depend on your market, your offer, and consistent execution.

Do I have to be on every platform?+

Definitely not. Your audience, strengths, and resources determine where you show up. A focused presence on the right channels beats being forgettable on all of them.

What does working together cost?+

That depends on the work. A brand strategy project and ongoing content support need different scopes. Share what you're trying to achieve and Ted will put together a proposal with clear deliverables and pricing.

What if I'm starting from scratch?+

Then we start with the foundation: who you serve, what makes you different, and why it matters. You don't need a big following to start building a brand worth following.
